# Research method and boundaries

The replay reads recorded Polymarket order-book snapshots alongside $p_{market,t}$, $p_{theory,t}$, imbalance, elapsed market time, and remaining settlement time. It reconstructs a fee-aware decision path using visible top-of-book / VWAP observations.

The relevant decision quantity is the post-cost edge:

$$
e_t = p_{theory,t} - a_t - f_t.
$$

Here $a_t$ is the executable ask for the selected side and $f_t$ is the fee estimate. A high model probability is not enough; $e_t$ must remain positive after execution costs.

## What this replay cannot prove

- Queue priority, message latency, cancellation races, partial fills, and adverse selection are not simulated exactly.
- The theoretical probability is evaluated from stored model inputs; it is not a counterfactual re-fit for every candidate.
- A parameter sweep makes selection bias easier, not harder. Its main use is to rule out fragile regions and explain risk changes.
